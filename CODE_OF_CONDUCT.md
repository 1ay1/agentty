# Code of Conduct

agentty follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Be kind and constructive.

Report abusive or harassing behaviour to the maintainers privately through a
[security advisory](https://github.com/agentty-org/agentty/security/advisories/new)
or to tfeayush@gmail.com. Every report is looked at, and the reporter's
privacy is respected.

Full text: https://agentty.org/code-of-conduct
